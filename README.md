# The Proven Package: install in 60 seconds

Gives a video its public face before you write a word of it. It takes this week's
winner file and the shapes your lane already proved, and hands back 3 title and
thumbnail pairs you lock. Every title is a clone of a shape that won, with one slot
swapped. Every thumbnail is built in layers with your real face, and any proof in the
picture is a real file, never generated.

Then the script gets written to the promise you locked, not the other way around.

## Install

Say this to Claude Code, in the folder your squad lives in:

    Install this skill: https://github.com/AI-ChrisLee/the-proven-package. Clone the whole folder into .claude/skills as the-proven-package, without the .git folder.

## What to bring

This week's winner file from the Winning Scrape (`squad/week/<date>-winner.md`), your
lane file, and your offer document. Two things the first run sets up with you if they
are missing: 2 to 4 photos of your face in `squad/face/`, and a free fal.ai key for
the image calls. No receipts on file and it interviews you for your real numbers right
there, and your answers become the bank every later title draws from.

## Run

    Run the proven package.

Three titles come back with a pass or fail on each of the 5 checks, and one ask before
any image is made: the cost, the endpoint, the one-time installs. Change any word that
does not sound like you and say yes once. It builds the images, opens a review sheet in
your browser, and changes exactly what you name. Then you say lock.

Already published a video that came in under your own median? Say **"repackage that
video"** with its link and its numbers, and the same cut gets one new pair.

## What you get

`squad/week/<date>-package.md`: the 3 locked pairs, the receipt behind every claim, the
5-check sheet, and the line that tells you what to do at upload. The images sit in
`squad/week/thumbs/<date>/`, finals plus their 1280x720 upload copies.

All 3 pairs ship. YouTube's Test & Compare takes up to 3 titles and thumbnails on one
video, free, and decides on watch time. You start that test in Studio the day you
upload.

The full procedure is `SKILL.md`. Stuck? Reply to the email that sent you here.
