# The Proven Package: install in 60 seconds

This skill is a base. Once you have done it your way, tell your squad "update the skill to do it like this."

Gives a video its public face before you write a word of it. It takes this week's winner
file and the shapes your lane already proved, and hands back 3 title and thumbnail pairs
you lock. Every title is a clone of a shape that won, with one slot swapped. Every
thumbnail is built in layers with your real face, and any proof in the picture is a real
file, never generated.

## Install

Say this to Claude Code, in the folder your squad lives in:

    Install this skill: https://github.com/AI-ChrisLee/the-proven-package. Clone the whole folder into .claude/skills as the-proven-package, without the .git folder.

## What to bring

This week's winner file from the Winning Scrape (`squad/week/<date>-winner.md`), your lane
file, and your offer document. The first run sets up whatever is missing: 2 to 4 photos of
your face in `squad/face/`, a free fal.ai key for the image calls, and your real numbers if
you have no receipts on file.

## Run

    Run the proven package.

3 titles come back, a main and 2 subs, with a pass or fail on each of the 5 checks, and one
ask before any image is made: the cost and the one-time installs. Change any word that does
not sound like you and say yes once. It builds the 3 thumbnails, opens a review sheet in
your browser, and changes exactly what you name. Then you say lock.

Published a video that came in under your own median? Say **"repackage that video"** with
its link and its numbers, and the same cut gets one new pair.

Test & Compare picked a winner? Say **"pair 2 won"** (or "the main pair won") with the
video's title. That shape goes into your lane file under "Your own winners", and from week
2 it takes the main pair.

## What you get

`squad/week/<date>-package.md`: the 3 locked pairs, the receipt behind every claim, the
5-check sheet, and the line that tells you what to do at upload. The images sit in
`squad/week/thumbs/<date>/`, finals plus their 1280x720 upload copies. It never uploads
anything.

The full procedure is `SKILL.md`. Stuck? Reply to the email that sent you here.
