# Thumbnail cages

The six proven thumbnail layouts, each extracted from a channel that wins with it;
every reference thumbnail was downloaded and measured, and every claim below links to
the real video. All five were proven on builder, AI-tool, or business channels, so in
another niche the overlap question is open: check it before pairing. This is the
skill's shipped copy: the first run seeds it to the roots file's thumb-cages path
(default `squad/thumb-cages.md`), and from there the founder grows it. **This file
grows:** when a weekly winner file shows a layout not in here, or when no cage's
proving channel sits in this founder's niche, distill a new cage from the thumbnails
the Winning Scrape downloaded for this lane, with its proof link. To see any cage's
reference, pull the reference thumbnail from the proof video:
`i.ytimg.com/vi/<video-id>/maxresdefault.jpg` (the id sits in the watch link).

Precedence: a cage's own written geometry is authoritative; the laws govern whatever
a cage does not specify. Model choice and cost rules live in the Proven Package
SKILL.md (one authority); this file's build section defers to it.

## The laws (measured across all 30 reference thumbnails)

1. **Nothing is small.** The largest non-face element runs 30-55% of the frame; a chart
   stroke is marker-thick (8-14px at 1280 wide); app icons run 15-17% of frame width
   (~200px); a proof phone/laptop runs 45-70% of frame height. Undersized elements are
   the most common first-draft failure.
2. **Text is 2-6 words and its primary line runs 14-28% of frame height** (100-200px at
   720p). If a line is under ~100px tall it does not survive the 320px mobile render,
   the width every pair is judged at.
3. **Text is never generated; it is composited**, supersampled 2x then downscaled, in a
   heavy grotesk (the house default: Inter Black, free on Google Fonts). The weight
   does the work; effects exist only to survive busy or dark grounds (hard black offset
   shadow, or a bottom-of-frame dark gradient to seat white text).
4. **The number is the headline** on every result video ($10K, 31 lbs, $500k/MO).
   Numbers never float as plain text on proof surfaces; on a UI they live in a badge,
   and the badge arrow points the direction the buyer wants the number to go.
5. **One accent color per thumbnail with a fixed meaning.** The accent here is the
   founder's ONE color, read off `squad/business.md`'s BRAND line (asked for when the
   document has no BRAND line) when this file is first seeded, then kept every week;
   #146ef5 is the source repo's example, not a default. Red only for negation, green
   only for the up-arrow, a tool's own logo colors only inside that logo. One accent
   word or one accent box, never two.
6. **Faces are chest-up, large (40-55% of frame height), direct eye contact**, and the
   expression matches the claim (intense for authority, smirk for insider, smile for a
   win). Cage 3 needs no face at all; every other cage's no-face fallback is the same
   geometry with the proof surface blown larger into the face zone, never an invented
   layout. When the real proof cannot be shown verbatim (client confidentiality), use
   a redacted REAL UI (blur names, keep the real chrome); a painted stand-in is slop.
7. **Proof beats decoration.** A real UI (revenue screen, App Store page, dashboard)
   blown up huge reads as a receipt; a painted scene reads as AI slop. Whatever the
   claim is about (a screen, a body, a physical result, a room, a piece of equipment)
   is a real file composited in code, never generated. Clean,
   flat, bright grounds win; dark-with-glow is the AI tell.
8. **Face photos come from `squad/face/`** (2-4 verified refs). No folder, no face
   thumbnails.

## Cage 1 · ANNOTATED CHART (Kallaway)

The whiteboard-marketer look: white grid paper, face dead-center chest-up at ~45% of
frame height, ONE continuous thick hand-drawn black chart line running edge to edge
behind the head, 3-4 LARGE rough accent-color circles/arrows annotating it, tiny
handwritten labels on the line. Text bottom-stacked: one word on an accent highlight
box (black text) plus one huge white word on a near-black bottom gradient; or 2-3 black
sentence-case words top-right with an arrow to the chart. 2-6 words, sentence case
beats caps.
When: a method/idea claim that a diagram can state literally.

## Cage 6 · MONO PORTRAIT (Nic Munoz, the house default from 2026-09-02)

One subject, black and white, on an off-white paper ground with a soft radial vignette.
The portrait (chest-up or bust, 55-65% of frame height) sits dead-center and fades into
the ground at the bottom through a short gradient (bottom 20% of the frame). Above it, a
2-3 word label in italic serif (Instrument Serif Italic, 14-18% of frame height, black),
centered, with a small hand-drawn black arrow curling from the label down to the
subject. When a number belongs on the frame it runs bottom-center in Inter Black at
~9% of frame height. **Black text on white, always** (Kellan's rule, measured on his
981K breakout). ONE thing carries the accent: one word of the label, or the number, or
one object in the frame, in the founder's color; everything else stays grey. No second
color, no badge, no glow. The subject can be the founder, a client, a screen, or the
artifact; the geometry does not change.
Why it is the default: a channel with nothing broken out yet gets more from one
recognisable frame across every upload than from five layouts, and this one is the
cheapest to make correctly (one photo, one word, one arrow). When the channel's own
breakouts show a different shape, the winner file overrides this default.
When: every upload until a breakout says otherwise. The label is the title's first
half, the "everyone's thing", never the money; the money, if present, is the bottom line.
Proof (channel @nic-munoz, 77.8K subscribers, 241 videos, read 2026-09-02):
"How A True Polymath Learns." 374K (4.8x the channel) · "How A True Workaholic Works"

## The build (house method)

Layers built apart, fused, text last:
1. Base layer: one image-model call (the endpoints, tiers, and prices live in the
   Proven Package SKILL.md; the quality tier is Nano Banana Pro,
   `fal-ai/gemini-3-pro-image-preview/edit`), face refs from `squad/face/` + the cage's
   reference thumbnail as a layout and scale anchor (layout and scale ONLY; every
   content element is named for the founder's niche in the prompt), "no words
   anywhere" in every prompt, element sizes stated in percent of frame, and every zone
   layer 2 composites (proof device, proof zone, quote card, badges, pills, list
   panels, straight arrows) rendered EMPTY.
2. Mechanical layers in code, crisp and never generated: panels, badges, pills, niche
   lists, quote cards, arrows that must be straight.
3. Type layer: composited at 2x then downscaled (Inter Black; Permanent Marker for the
   casual qualifier; Caveat for handwriting labels; all three fetched once per repo
   into `squad/fonts/` by the SKILL.md recipe, and a missing font blocks the layer
   rather than swapping), sized by law 2.
4. Evaluate at 320px width against the cage's reference thumbnail side by side; if the
   reference reads stronger, fix scale first, then contrast, then wording.
